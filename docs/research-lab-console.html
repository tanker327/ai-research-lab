    Best Local Coding Model for a Single RTX Pro 6000 (96 GB)

    synthesized 10:1523 approved claims47 live evidence items2 evaluation cycles

    Executive summary

    Across four credible candidates, Qwen3.6-27B (FP8) is the strongest practical choice for this hardware and these priorities. It leads or ties on agentic coding benchmarks, fits comfortably in 96 GB with headroom for 128K context, and is the only candidate with independently validated results on both coding-quality metrics in scope. Kimi-K2.5-Coder-42B is the quality ceiling but sacrifices context headroom; DeepSeek-V4-Lite-33B is the value pick with a weaker agentic profile.

    Recommendation

    Run Qwen3.6-27B FP8 as the primary local coding model. Keep Kimi-K2.5-Coder-42B (INT4) available for maximum-quality passes where 32K context suffices.

    Why not the others

    GLM-5-Coder-32B posts a competitive vendor benchmark, but the figure could not be independently reproduced — the community replication came in 6.8 points lower under matched settings, and the claim remains marked contested in the evidence base. Kimi-K2.5-Coder wins raw quality but its practical 32K context ceiling on this GPU conflicts with the agentic-workflow priority, where long context does real work.
